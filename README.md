## About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at **Florida International University**, working in stochastic analysis and numerical methods for partial differential equations.

My research focuses on **stochastic partial differential equations (SPDEs)**, particularly stochastic fluid models, phase-field equations, long-time behavior, numerical approximation, synchronization, and data assimilation.

My current work includes stochastic Boussinesq/Navier–Stokes systems and stochastic Allen–Cahn equations with dynamic boundary conditions.

---

## Research

### 🌊 Stochastic Boussinesq & Navier–Stokes Equations

Theory and numerical analysis of stochastic fluid equations, with particular interest in:

* stochastic forcing and energy estimates,
* numerical approximation,
* long-time stability and invariant measures,
* continuous data assimilation,
* and computational simulation of stochastic fluid systems.

**→ [Research repository](BOUSSINESQ_REPOSITORY_LINK)**

---

### Stochastic Allen–Cahn Synchronization

Coupled stochastic Allen–Cahn systems with dynamic boundary conditions and multiplicative noise.

Current topics include:

* variational well-posedness,
* synchronization of coupled stochastic systems,
* dynamic boundary conditions,
* implicit numerical approximation,
* stability and convergence,
* and computational experiments.

**→ [Research repository](ALLEN_CAHN_REPOSITORY_LINK)**
